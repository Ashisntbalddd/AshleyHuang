My story

Hello!

My name is Ashley Huang. I’m a community college student in California exploring the intersection of chemistry, biology, computation, and drug discovery.

My path into science has been shaped by curiosity more than by a single subject. I enjoy understanding how molecules and biological systems work, but I’m equally interested in what computation can add to that process — especially protein analysis, mutation studies, machine learning, and emerging AI tools for scientific research.

Outside the lab and classroom, I draw, sing, and play Go. I like work that combines logic with creativity, and I hope to keep building a life where both have a place.

— Ashley
