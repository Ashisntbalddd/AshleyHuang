About Me

Hello!

My name is Ashley Huang. I’m from Southern California, and I love anything related to biochemistry, math, art, and coding.

I’m naturally curious and always trying something new. Whether I’m learning a new scientific concept, working through a math problem, creating art, experimenting with code, or exploring a new research idea, I enjoy challenging myself and seeing where curiosity can take me.

I’m especially interested in the places where different fields connect. I love the logic of math and programming, the complexity of biochemistry, and the freedom of art. To me, learning is not just about choosing one subject — it’s about finding unexpected connections between them.

I hope to keep exploring, creating, and learning throughout my life, while becoming someone who is never afraid to ask questions and try something unfamiliar.
